Player photos for the TikTok share picture: `<persona-id>.jpg` (littler, rvb, humphries, cross),
portrait or square, the face in the upper third. Put the credit line in `credits.json`:

    { "littler": "Foto: <author>, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons" }

Photos are not committed (see .gitignore): only use ones you have the right to use, and keep the
credit the licence asks for. They are only used in the videos made by `record.mjs`, never on the site.
