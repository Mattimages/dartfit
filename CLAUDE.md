# DartFit — notes for working in this repo

Mobile site: measure the throwing hand next to a bank card, fit darts to it, link to affiliate shops. EU-wide (30 countries), NL/EN/DE, steel tip.

## Rules

- No image leaves the device. Only measurements (mm) are sent to the API. Keep it that way.
- No secrets in the browser. The Anthropic key, SMTP and affiliate IDs live on the server.
- Every numeric constant in `packages/engine/src/config.ts` needs a provenance entry; a test enforces it. Bump `ENGINE_VERSION` when fitting output changes.
- The AI explanation may only state numbers present in its input; `apps/api/src/explain.ts` checks this and falls back to the template.
- Strict CSP: no inline style attributes (set `el.style.cssText` via the `h()` helper), no inline scripts, fonts and models self-hosted.
- Only affiliate shops are linked (retailers with `affiliateEnv`; in production the ID must be set). No affiliate link → the dart is not shown. A-Z Darts is a spec source only.
- Results show the best fit per price class (budget ≤ €50, standard ≤ €80, premium), see `priceTiers` and `packages/engine/src/money.ts`.
- Height enters via `packages/engine/src/body.ts` (the forearm is estimated from height; the questionnaire no longer asks it): hand estimate without a scan, scan cross-check, and launch-angle physics that sets throw style when the player says "not sure".
- Product photos live in `packages/catalogue/data/photos.csv` and are served from our own origin through the API's `/img/<model>` proxy (CSP keeps `img-src 'self'`; visitors never contact an image CDN). All photos are shown (`PREVIEW_PHOTOS` defaults to true, owner's decision 22 Oct); `cleared` marks which come from an affiliate feed, and `PREVIEW_PHOTOS=false` limits the site to those.
- The result's product box has two views: **Foto** and **Model match** (the dart drawn over the player's ideal barrel, with `silhouetteOverlap` as a %). The choice is site-wide (`apps/web/src/media.ts`).
- Design system: 17px root (rem scales), outlined ghost buttons, underlined link buttons. Geist, hairlines (1px `--line`), radii `--r-ctl` 12 / `--r-tile` 16 / `--r-card` 20 / `--r-stage` 24, weights 500–650, tight negative tracking on headings and numbers.
- Each result dart shows three columns (`views/match-split.ts`, engine `matchBreakdown`): shape match (weight, length, thickness), style match (type, knurling, balance) and the total, which is the usual match score; the parts use the same curve with their share of `match.weights`. The landing film shows the same three numbers (and ranks by the total) after it fills in the hold and grip questions.
- The questionnaire asks shop country, height, hand type (only when the proportions weren't scanned: gift, no-card height only; `Answers.handType` slender/average/stubby, engine 3.4 turns it into proportions so it nudges the shape like a scan), hold, fingers, throw and grip (steel tip only; no thickness, hand-moisture, current-dart or price-class questions: the engine uses `thickness: 'auto'` and `moisture: 'normal'`, and the result always shows all three price classes). Its live dart (`views/quiz-dart.ts`) follows every answer the way the engine reads it: hold spot and finger zone (`gripZone`), barrel shape for that hold, shaft and flight (`setupFor`), knurling from the grip slider. The landing film's question step uses the same dart.
- After the questionnaire, `views/fitting.ts` plays the landing film's ending with the player's own result (`castFor` with the real result pinned and 2 near misses, `fitFilm`; while the fit loads, three darts land on a board): first the email sign-up (once per visit, "Nu niet" skips; the fit is calculated meanwhile), then their ideal dart, a score carousel of three real darts (big names under the barrels) rising to their best match, darts landing on a real board (on segments that add up to the match %, `draw/checkout.ts`, no calculation shown) and "95% match", then their price options with "Je gooit als: <pro>" and that pro's player card dropping in above it (both films and the result show that pro; while the other price classes come up the card shrinks to a badge and the cards show only the total); it ends on "See my darts". No hand or answer replay there.
- The landing film is a link to the scan. Home offers the gift icon button; the scan page (after tapping the link) offers both icon buttons (`draw/icons.ts`): "Geen pasje?" (opens the no-card warning; `#/scan?nocard=1` still works) and "Koop je voor iemand anders?" (`#/gift`, `views/share.ts` `giftChoiceView`: either send the player a link `#/?cadeau=1&van=<name>` (home shows a banner, `session.giftRequest` survives the start, and the result leads with "Stuur je resultaat naar <name>"), or estimate it yourself: no scan, `session.gift`, the questions ask about "the player" via `.gift` copy keys, hold and fingers get "Weet ik niet").
- "Je gooit als" page (`views/pro-match.ts`, `#/fit/<id>/pro`, opened from the result's pro card): every pro ranked with `scoreVariant` against the player's fit, the top one's dart next to the ideal (table), what they share, the next three pros (linked to their guide pages) and a share box. Pros get a player card (`draw/pro-art.ts`: country colours, initials like MvG, a generic thrower), also on the result's pro card and the share picture; deliberately no faces or photos of players (portrait rights, and we'd be guessing likenesses).
- Sharing (`views/share.ts`): the result has a share box (phone share sheet with a 1080×1350 picture card, else WhatsApp and copy). Shared links `#/s?d=<variant>&m=<pct>&p=<pro>&g=1` carry only the dart, the % and the pro (never hand sizes or the fit id) and open a read-only page with that dart, its shop button and "your turn". Social previews use `public/og.png`.
- TikTok/Reels videos: `scripts/tiktok/record.mjs` records the preview build at 1080×1920 (CDP screencast with `--force-device-scale-factor=3`, the page clock slowed 4× for smooth frames): landing film, the questions tapped in, fitting film, result, "Je gooit als" page, then a share picture. Personas per pro come from `scripts/tiktok/search.ts`. A player photo is used only there, from `scripts/tiktok/photos/<id>.jpg` with a credit in `credits.json` (gitignored; never on the site).
- Motion ads (the main TikTok/Reels format): `#/ad?L=&h=&hold=&f=&throw=&g=` in the demo build only (`views/ad.ts`; production drops it via the `import.meta.env.MODE` check). About 19 s: hook ("Gooi jij met de verkeerde dart?"), hand scan, three taps, the match % climbing over three real darts, the pro's card slamming in, "Welke prof ben jij?" + findyourdarts.com. Numbers come from the real engine for each persona. `scripts/tiktok/ad.mjs` renders it (VIDEO_LANG nl/en/de).
- No ruler option on the site (`#/manual` stays only for the embedded preview, which has no hand model).
- No card at hand: the scan page offers a no-card scan behind a clear warning (`scanner.ts` `noCard`, `unscaledResult`). It measures in pixels and keeps only the proportions (`Measurements.unscaled`); the engine sizes the hand from height (else the reference hand) with that height spread, so bands widen and confidence drops (engine 3.3). The measurement screen shows only the finger/palm proportion, the height question and the result page say it is less precise.
- The hand detector heals itself (`scan/detector.ts`): after repeated errors, or 10 s without a hand while frames come in, it is rebuilt (a stuck first load used to need a page reload).
- "Keep me posted" is asked at the start of the fitting film and again at the end of the result (no pop-up on buy buttons; after signing up this visit the end box only says it's on, `session.alertsSent`) (`apps/web/src/views/alert-signup.ts`): direct opt-in, no confirmation link (owner's decision 24 Oct), through `/api/auth/request` with `purpose: 'alerts'`; it never signs in and only attaches the fit to a new address; it also mails an optional 24-hour link ("confirm your email to keep your account", `confirm.*`) that signs in and saves the fit, and an alert goes out only when a new dart becomes that person's best match (`jobs.ts`). Mail needs `SMTP_URL`; without it messages only land in the `outbox` table.
- The hand drawing (`draw/art.ts`) is traced from the 21 landmarks; skin tone comes from CSS custom properties (`SKIN_TONES`), and the landing film picks a new one each loop.
- US visitors (country `US`) get Darting.com as their shop (`darting_us`, affiliate ID in `DARTING_AFF_ID`, added as `AffId`) and a Darting.com partner card on the home and result pages (`views/partner.ts`, link `/go/partner:darting` with `AFFBanId=10025`). Built in our own markup: never paste partner banner scripts (CSP, privacy). Darting links go to the homepage until its search URL is confirmed.
- Amazon: only stores with a tag are linked. amazon.nl (NL, BE), amazon.co.uk (GB, IE), amazon.de (DE and the rest of the EU, as it ships EU-wide), amazon.com (US); tags in `AMAZON_TAG_NL/UK/DE/US` on Vercel. amazon.fr/it/es/se/pl/com.be have no tag and no countries; add both together.
- Buy buttons show Amazon only: `buyButtons: ["amazon"]` in `retailers.json` filters both the API offers and the browser bundle; the other affiliate shops stay configured (drop or extend the list to bring them back).
- Country (shops) and language are separate; see `apps/web/src/region.ts`. No pop-up on the first visit: the questionnaire's first step asks "Waar koop je je darts?" (six popular countries, the browser's guess pre-selected, plus a list with the rest; `views/questions.ts`); the picker (`views/region-picker.ts`, a modal) opens from the globe in the header; search knows English and Dutch names too. German lives in `i18n.de.ts` and falls back to English.
- SEO / AI search: guide pages are real HTML (not hash routes) built at `vite build` by `apps/web/seo` (content per language in `content.{nl,en,de}.ts`, rendered by `render.ts`): a hub, eleven guides (`content.*.ts`, `topics.ts`: barrel shapes, grip, tungsten; `events.ts`: darts calendar with sourced dates, WDF rules, and a checkout table generated by `src/draw/checkout.ts`) and a page per pro (`pros.ts` + `pro-pages.ts`, from pros.json and the catalogue, `/nl/profs/<name>/`, `/en/pros/…`, `/de/profis/…`) per language, plus `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` (AI crawlers allowed, `/api` and `/go` not) and `llms.txt`. Each page opens with a direct answer, uses question headings and a FAQ, and carries Article/FAQPage/Breadcrumb JSON-LD (a data block, fine under the CSP). Catalogue numbers in the text are `{placeholders}` filled from the live catalogue, so they can't go stale; `test/guides.test.ts` checks titles, descriptions, hreflang, CSP and phrases. Canonical host: `SITE_URL` (default findyourdarts.com, the Strato domain pointed at Vercel; dartfit.vercel.app and www redirect there, except `/api`). Keep claims honest (no study links hand size to the perfect weight); bump `UPDATED` when the guides change.
- Bing & co.: IndexNow key file `/<INDEXNOW_KEY>.txt` and a ping with every guide URL at the end of each production build (`apps/web/vite.config.ts`, never fails the build; `INDEXNOW=off` disables). Bing feeds ChatGPT search, Copilot and DuckDuckGo. Google: `public/googledad782361da4832a.html` verifies Search Console.
- Vercel Web Analytics (`@vercel/analytics`, cookieless): hash routes are sent as paths with ids replaced (`/fit/[id]`), guide pages load `/_vercel/insights/script.js`; Web Analytics must be enabled in the Vercel project. No two-week "which dart did you pick" opt-in any more (only an off switch for people who had it).
- Copy lives in `apps/web/src/i18n.ts`, NL and EN side by side. Write plain Dutch; no "je/u" mixing (use je). Say "pasje" (het pasje), not "bankpas".

## Engine v3 in one paragraph

Hand dimensions and answers become Gaussian evidence for weight, length and diameter (`fit.ts`). `enforcePhysics` projects the target onto what a real barrel can be: mass from `barrel.ts` (alloy density, bore, piecewise-linear profile, machining factor 0.876 calibrated on 34 straight catalogue barrels, about ±7% IQR). If 90% tungsten can't make it, 95% is tried. Shape starts from hold and throw, then the measured hand nudges it (`handShapeNudge`, config `handShape`): short fingers for the palm and a compact grip lean towards bomb/torpedo, long fingers spread along the barrel towards straight; estimated hands (height) don't nudge. `solveIdeal` then picks taper lengths for the wanted mass and balance, leaning towards the preferred shape's reference profile and moving the diameter within `handShape.diameterFlex` to keep the weight; the ideal is labelled by its nearest reference shape. `recommend()` ranks catalogue darts by band fit and excludes listings whose stated weight fails the physics check (`specCheck`).
